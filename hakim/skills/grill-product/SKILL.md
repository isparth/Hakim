# grill-product

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of the **product** plan until you reach genuine
shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between
decisions one by one.

- Ask the questions **one at a time**.
- For each question, **provide your recommended answer** — take a position, don't just interrogate.
- If a question can be answered by reading the existing docs (`prd.md`) or the codebase, **go look
  instead of asking**.

Focus on the product, not the implementation:
- Who is the core user, and what is the *one* job this has to do well for them?
- What's genuinely MVP vs. what's later vs. what you're deliberately NOT doing?
- Where do features overlap, conflict, or depend on each other?
- What does success look like — how will you know it worked?

Keep going until the doubts are gone and you and the user are aligned. This is the mechanism for
clearing confusion before a product decision is locked — reach for it throughout the project, not
just at the start. Alignment now beats rework later.
